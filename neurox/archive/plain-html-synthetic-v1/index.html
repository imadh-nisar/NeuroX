    Limits of the data

    • Under-reporting in rural clinics can make need look lower than it is. Reporting completeness is shown beside each index value.
    • Years and districts with no reported data are shown as a gap, never filled with an estimate.
    • Districts are placed at approximate centres. Official boundaries need a licensed GeoJSON file.
    • Alerts depend on a short history, so they are prompts to check the source, not conclusions.
